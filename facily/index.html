Company profile

Software shaped by self-storage operations.

Facily.ai is an early-stage self-storage technology company led by CEO and Co-Founder Jared Mastroianni. It is developing operator-focused software around accountable workflows, evidence, and decision support.

What Facily.ai is

An early-stage company, presented without inflated claims.

Facily.ai is being built for self-storage operators and teams. Its product direction is informed by facility-level work and multi-facility operating needs.

The company does not publish customer, revenue, funding, integration, security-certification, or performance claims without current evidence. It also does not present roadmap concepts as commercially available features.

Entity boundary

Company and product are not interchangeable.

Facily.ai is the company identity. Facily OS is the working product identity for the operating platform under development.

The final legal owner, creator, and provider identifiers will be published in structured data after governing company and product records are reconciled. Until then, the site avoids ownership language that could create conflicting entities.
