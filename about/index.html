Executive profile

An operator’s view of technology.

Jared Mastroianni works across two distinct roles: operating leadership at modSTORAGE and company building at Facily.ai. The common thread is disciplined execution—making work clearer, decisions better supported, and outcomes easier to verify.

Biography

Operations first. Software with a reason.

Jared Mastroianni is the Chief Operating Officer of modSTORAGE and the CEO and Co-Founder of Facily.ai. He works at the intersection of self-storage operations, technology strategy, and facility-level execution.

At modSTORAGE, his perspective is shaped by the systems that help a geographically distributed portfolio execute consistently: accountable work, customer experience, training, financial controls, reporting, maintenance, and property operations.

At Facily.ai, Jared applies that operating perspective to the development of software for self-storage teams. His product standard is straightforward: a system should distinguish a recommendation from a fact, internal activity from an external result, and a roadmap from a released capability.

This profile states current executive roles provided by Jared and supported by current internal business records. Product capabilities and performance results are published only with release or measurement evidence.
