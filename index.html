Executive profile

Operational discipline is the product strategy.

Jared’s work is grounded in the practical demands of operating self-storage facilities: making responsibilities visible, resolving exceptions, protecting financial truth, supporting staff, and improving the customer experience.

At modSTORAGE, that means building repeatable operating controls across a geographically distributed portfolio. At Facily.ai, it means translating those lessons into software principles and an early-stage operating platform for the industry.

Product principles

Accountable technology for real operating decisions.

These are design principles, not performance claims. Public capability statements will be tied to current release evidence.

01

Evidence before automation

A recommendation is not a fact, and an activity is not a reconciled result. Operating software should show the difference.

02

Operators shape the product

Useful facility software begins with real exceptions, handoffs, approvals, and accountability—not a generic dashboard.

03

Status is part of the truth

Available, beta, limited release, in development, planned, and conceptual capabilities should never be presented as interchangeable.
