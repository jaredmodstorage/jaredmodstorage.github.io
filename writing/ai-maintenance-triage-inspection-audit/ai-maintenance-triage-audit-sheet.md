# AI Maintenance Triage Audit Sheet

## Define the batch

Record the facilities, report window, authorized inspection window, eligible cosmetic category, unit of analysis, excluded categories and their owners, model version, cutoff, inspection checklist version and staff capacity. Set minimum inspection completion, minimum coverage by band and the decision the results could authorize.

| Field | Record |
| --- | --- |
| Snapshot | Facility, public area, report or defect ID, received time, eligible category |
| Model result | Version, score, band or abstention, cutoff, ordinary queue decision |
| Audit draw | Group, group size, planned draw size, selected IDs, draw method and reference |
| Field review | Reviewer, inspection time, evidence reference, qualifying, nonqualifying or unknown |
| Work decision | Authorized owner, action and date. Preserve whether a visit served normal work, the audit or both. |

## Work through the audit

- [ ] Freeze the eligible roster before inspection outcomes. Link duplicate reports while preserving the draw record.
- [ ] Draw cases from each group. Keep the score off the inspection form when feasible.
- [ ] Check each selected case within the window or record why it remains unresolved. Escalate urgent or service-affecting findings through the established procedure.
- [ ] Keep work authorization separate from the evaluation record. Withhold the evaluation cases from training while they serve as the evaluation set.

## Reconcile findings

| Group | Record and interpret |
| --- | --- |
| Above cutoff | Eligible, selected, inspected, qualifying, nonqualifying, unresolved. |
| Below cutoff | Same fields by band. A complete simple random draw estimates qualifying count by multiplying the group size by the sample’s qualifying fraction. Combine estimated counts across groups. |
| Unscored | Keep abstentions visible. Reconcile the inspection outcomes and unknowns separately. |
| Missing outcomes | Report the reason and show extreme-case bounds for selected cases. Keep sampling uncertainty separate. |

## Fictional example

| Group | Selection and result |
| --- | --- |
| Above cutoff | 20 of 20 inspected. 16 qualify. |
| Below cutoff | 20 of 100 selected randomly. All inspected. Five qualify. The estimated group count is 25. |
| Unscored | All four selected. Three inspected. One qualifies, two do not and one is unresolved. |

Among scored cases, the fictional estimated share of qualifying reports below cutoff is 25 ÷ (25 + 16), about 61%. The five qualifying field findings can enter normal work planning. The estimated total stays in the evaluation record. No confidence interval or operating outcome is reported.

**Decision record.** Enter the permitted change or hold, the remaining uncertainty, the person who owns the decision and the date of the next audit.
