# Originality and claim-boundary review

## Originality

The distinct architectural insight is that AI operating authority belongs to an exact release tuple—not a model family, endpoint, alias, or general capability. A changed model becomes a candidate whose action ceiling is downgraded until evidence and approval are bound to its model, prompt, retrieval, tool, policy, configuration, and evaluation identities.

The title, `model-version-action-authority-self-storage` slug, Juniper Lock Storage example, AI Release Authority Binding Record, 56-field registers, and body passed public, calendar, assignment, current-day, and prior-package checks. A normalized 14-word-sequence comparison found zero exact body matches across 37 prior articles.

## Claim boundaries

- Juniper Lock Storage and every related facility, model, prompt, policy, tool, system, person, identifier, time, test, record, decision, and outcome are explicitly fictional.
- No real customer, facility, employee, provider, model deployment, product capability, partnership, certification, adoption, performance result, revenue effect, occupancy effect, award, coverage, or recognition is claimed.
- The fictional runtime mismatch, downgrade, evaluation, approval, and receipt are teaching records—not evidence of a Facily.ai or modSTORAGE implementation.
- NIST AI RMF material is voluntary and cross-sectoral. AI RMF 1.0 is being revised; the article does not represent it as a self-storage rule or certification.
- NIST AI 600-1 supports context-aware pre-deployment testing and its limitations. It does not guarantee validity or authorize any operational action.
- NIST SP 800-218A is a secure-development community profile used with SP 800-218; it is not a model assessment or deployment approval.
- NIST SP 800-53 Revision 5.1 is a general security/privacy control catalog requiring tailoring; it is not an AI-specific self-storage mandate or compliance conclusion.
- Availability is not authority; test completion is not approval; tool invocation is not side-effect completion; model output is not observed facility fact; a release receipt is not an operating result.

## State separation

Local completion, publisher handoff, queue placement, repository copy, commit, push, provider deployment, public HTTP availability, canonical activation, feed or sitemap inclusion, crawling, indexing, reach, coverage, recognition, and result remain separate states.
