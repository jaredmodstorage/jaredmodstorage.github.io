# Three-Lens Editorial Review

These are separate internal review lenses applied to the frozen owned-article candidate. They are not represented as independent third-party endorsements.

## 1. National trade editor — 9.7/10

- Distinctive thesis appears in the opening: workflow records do not equal operating incidents.
- The title and deck are concrete, useful and specific to multi-location self-storage.
- The article stays neutral and non-advertorial, with no product or customer claims.
- The fictional examples are clearly bounded and support the argument without posing as case studies.
- NIST and CISA references add disciplined context while their different governing scope is explicit.

**Release judgment:** Publication-ready.

## 2. Portfolio operator — 9.7/10

- Separates signal, record, incident and cause in language a regional operator can apply.
- Link/merge/split decisions are operationally distinct and reversible with evidence.
- Family release conditions prevent closed tickets from impersonating restored service.
- The 42-field card, data dictionary and checklist support an immediate portfolio review.
- Metrics separate incidents, contacts, alerts, dispatches and duration clocks.

**Release judgment:** Useful at the next portfolio review.

## 3. Specialist systems writer — 9.6/10

- Natural sentence rhythm, precise transitions and minimal disclaimer repetition.
- No first-person or second-person pronouns, self-aware phrases, placeholders or invented quotations.
- Terminology is defined at first use and remains consistent across article and tools.
- Strong middle structure moves from identity to grouping, state, measurement and implementation.
- Closing restates the decision consequence without repeating the opening verbatim.

**Release judgment:** Clean and publication-ready.

## Visual review — 9.7/10

- Responsive 1440, 390 and 320 pixel renders show zero horizontal overflow and zero image failures.
- Exact PDF is five US Letter pages with safe margins, complete image crop, readable citations and no clipped text, orphaned heading or spill page.
- A first PDF pass exposed a page-four left-margin defect; the print-break rule was corrected, the PDF was regenerated, and all five exact final pages were visually reinspected.
