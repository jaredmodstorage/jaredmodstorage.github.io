# Originality, rights and claim boundary

## Distinct contribution

This article addresses whether the same AI score and threshold can support the same operating conclusion across self-storage facilities. Its controlling object is a **site-level calibration record** joining score meaning, outcome definition, evaluation population, threshold evidence, error costs, human authority, drift and correction.

It does not repeat the governed work on denominators, reproducible portfolio benchmarks, temporal authority, observation coverage, semantic drift, intervention evidence, model-score handback or the Facility Work Graph. Those works may help establish inputs or operating context; none resolves whether a model output has the same empirical and operational meaning at different facilities.

## Rights and example boundary

- The manuscript and companion checklist were created for Jared Mastroianni's owned editorial program.
- No third-party manuscript, accepted assignment or exclusive route is reused.
- Quotations are avoided. The article paraphrases only the bounded points listed in `source-register.csv` and links to each official source.
- Ridgefield Storage Group, its nine facilities, all scores, thresholds, work orders, label differences and review outcomes are fictional.
- No real model, product, customer, facility, employee, vendor, deployment, accuracy, performance improvement, revenue, occupancy, savings, award, recognition or coverage is claimed.

## Technical boundary

- A raw score is not called a probability unless the model documentation and evaluation evidence support that meaning.
- Calibration, ranking performance, threshold metrics, fairness review and operating approval remain separate.
- The article does not prescribe a calibration algorithm or represent NIST, Google or scikit-learn guidance as a self-storage rule.
- Model output remains proposed analytical evidence, not an observed facility fact or permission for consequential action.
- Human review and release authority must remain proportionate to the actual decision.
