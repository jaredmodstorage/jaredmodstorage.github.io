Pending AI Decision Revalidation Register

Compare the conditions recorded when an AI proposal entered review with the current facility, evidence, policy and authority state before release.

Use boundary: This is a working template, not evidence that any system was tested. The four examples below are entirely fictional and unexecuted. A blank or selected field does not prove a control passed.

Release sequence

  1. 1. Resolve the targetConfirm stable organization, facility and target identifiers.
  2. 2. Load the snapshotRead the proposal-time evidence, target, policy, authority and AI-build references.
  3. 3. Read current stateQuery the named authoritative sources rather than a cached answer.
  4. 4. Compare material fieldsApply the decision class's triggers and expiration window.
  5. 5. Record one dispositionRelease, recompute, hand back or expire with a named reason.
  6. 6. Prevent duplicate actionVerify that the same approved operation did not already occur.
