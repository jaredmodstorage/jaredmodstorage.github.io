# Originality and claim-boundary check

## Original contribution

The package treats cancellation as a versioned withdrawal of action authority that must reach queued and executing copies before a target-specific commit boundary. Its practical contribution is the combined pattern of durable revocation, fast propagation, authoritative pre-commit recheck, explicit race states, target readback and evidence-based handback.

The framing is distinct from model-output accuracy, general human approval, source correction, access lifecycle, retries, shadow mode, release governance, agent delegation and generic observability.

## Fictional content

- Briar Junction Self Storage is fictional.
- All people, systems, vendors, identifiers, timestamps, events, receipts and results in the scenario and CSV are fictional.
- The example demonstrates record structure and state reasoning only. It is not a customer, deployment, test result or performance claim.

## Claims not made

The article does not claim:

- a real customer, facility, vendor, integration, deployment, model or product;
- improved revenue, occupancy, cost, response time, safety, compliance or reliability;
- universal support for cancellation, queue removal, worker interruption, idempotency queries or compensating action;
- that a local API response proves target completion or non-completion;
- NIST, W3C, CNCF, OpenTelemetry or provider certification, endorsement or compliance;
- that the recommended design satisfies any specific legal, security, privacy, safety, vendor or contractual requirement; or
- that revocation automatically reverses an action that already crossed its commit boundary.

## Tool boundary

The AI Action Revocation Propagation Test is an architecture and operations diagnostic. Teams must adapt it to their authorized systems, provider contracts, action types, evidence capabilities and applicable policies. It does not perform a live revocation, prove that a target side effect is absent, select a legal remedy or authorize a recovery action.

## Source boundary

CloudEvents informs envelope identity, not business revocation semantics. OpenTelemetry Context informs correlation, not authorization. PROV-O informs provenance representation, not an execution protocol. NIST AI RMF and SP 800-53 provide voluntary risk-management and control context, not a self-storage implementation mandate or certification.
