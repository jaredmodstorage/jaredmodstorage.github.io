# Restroom Backup Boundary, Service and Reopening Card

Use this card with current company policy, site procedures, product labels, safety data sheets and applicable requirements. It is an operating prompt, not a universal cleanup method or a substitute for qualified plumbing, sanitation, electrical, environmental, safety or legal advice.

## First five minutes

- [ ] Stop use of the affected fixture and other fixtures identified by the responsible owner or provider.
- [ ] Close and mark a precise controlled area; protect adjacent walking and customer routes.
- [ ] Do not flush again, test another fixture or touch electrical equipment in or beside liquid.
- [ ] Do not mix cleaners, add chemicals, mask odor or begin an unapproved cleanup.
- [ ] Record the discovery time, direct observations, visible liquid boundary and who established control.
- [ ] Escalate any contact with electrical equipment, customer belongings or spaces outside the restroom.

## Continuity and service handoff

- [ ] Confirm the employee restroom continuity plan and decision owner.
- [ ] Verify any customer accommodation or accessibility decision with the authorized owner.
- [ ] Create one incident record with facility ID, event ID and named owner.
- [ ] Assign separate scopes for plumbing, sanitation and other building systems as needed.
- [ ] Give each provider the address, access contact, observed condition and controlled boundary.
- [ ] Record provider/company, arrival window, check-in, completed scope and follow-up needs.

## Release gate

Keep the area closed until the assigned decision owner verifies:

- [ ] Source or flow condition corrected within the plumbing provider's scope.
- [ ] Affected surfaces cleaned and sanitized under the approved method and scope.
- [ ] Waste and contaminated disposable materials removed through the approved process.
- [ ] Floor and threshold dry, stable and free of residue or a slip hazard.
- [ ] Contacted electrical components or building materials evaluated when applicable.
- [ ] Toilet, sink, soap and hand-drying provisions ready for the room's approved use.
- [ ] Service records attached; unresolved exceptions assigned with owners and deadlines.
- [ ] Reopening authority, timestamp and basis recorded.
