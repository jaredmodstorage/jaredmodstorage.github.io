# Payment-Terminal Tamper Hold and Handoff Card

Use when a card-present terminal, reader or connected attachment does not match the facility's approved condition. This card does not diagnose a skimmer, determine a breach, replace the operator's incident plan or authorize staff to handle the device.

## Immediate hold

- [ ] Stop card-present transactions on the suspect device.
- [ ] Place the approved out-of-service notice before the card-present point.
- [ ] Keep customers, employees and vendors from touching or using the device.
- [ ] Do not pull, open, unplug, restart, power down or remove anything unless a qualified responder directs the action.
- [ ] Use a separate trusted device to contact the approved response chain.
- [ ] If there is an immediate life-safety hazard, call 911 and follow the emergency plan.

## Release gate

- [ ] Inspector or technician identity and authorization verified through an existing trusted contact.
- [ ] Device identity matches the approved register and provider record.
- [ ] Required provider, processor, security, legal and incident-response checks are complete or formally assigned.
- [ ] Evidence and required records are preserved in the approved location.
- [ ] Release owner, time and supporting evidence are recorded.
- [ ] Device state set to **released** or **replaced**; remaining communications, monitoring or investigation work stays open separately.

**Customer line:** "This terminal is temporarily unavailable while we verify the equipment. Please do not insert, tap or swipe a card here. I can help you use our approved alternative payment option."
