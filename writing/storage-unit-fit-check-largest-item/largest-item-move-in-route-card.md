# Route Card for Bulky Items

Use one card per assigned unit and route. Record measurements in a single unit of measure.

| Field | Record |
| --- | --- |
| Reservation | Facility, date, move-in window, assigned unit, floor and route |
| Largest item | Outside width, depth and height as moved, including packaging and added equipment |
| Handling | Restrictions from the mover or customer, dimension source and measurement date |
| Contact | Staff owner, next action and follow-up time |

## Walk in move-in order

- [ ] Confirm the approved unloading location and that the planned route will be available.
- [ ] Measure entrances, tight turns and usable space on both sides of each turn.
- [ ] Record elevator opening and cab separately. Confirm site rules and who checks the intended load.
- [ ] Check the assigned unit’s clear opening, threshold and placement space. Stop for a door that operates abnormally.

| Route point | Record |
| --- | --- |
| Unload and entry | Location, operating hours, clear opening width and height |
| Hall and turns | Narrow point, turn space on both sides and fixed projections |
| Elevator, if used | Clear door width and height, usable interior width, depth and height, approach and exit |
| Assigned unit | Unit number, clear opening width and height, threshold and room inside |
| Measurement record | Date, initials, outstanding checks and changes that require remeasurement |

## Give the customer a status

| Status | Next action |
| --- | --- |
| Known obstruction | Identify the measured conflict. Offer another route or unit for review when available. |
| Measurements available | Share the exact measurements. Ask the customer or mover to assess the move and review any close clearance. |
| Fit unresolved | Name the missing measurement or decision, who owns it and when they will follow up. |

**Fictional example.** Upright, wrapped cabinet: 41 inches wide, 37 deep and 73 high. Unit door clear width: 36 inches. Status: known obstruction for the stated handling plan. An alternate unit’s opening measures 47 inches wide and 84 high. Share the dimensions and confirm the complete alternate route, availability and terms before changing the assignment.
