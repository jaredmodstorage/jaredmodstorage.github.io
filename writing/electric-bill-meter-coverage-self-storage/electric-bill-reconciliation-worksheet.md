# Electric Bill Reconciliation Worksheet

Complete one record for each site, meter or verified aggregate and service period. Use your internal account reference and keep source bills in approved storage.

## Capture the bill and the meter

| Evidence | Record |
| --- | --- |
| Scope | Facility ID, area or loads served, effective dates, facilities verifier and evidence reference |
| Service identity | Utility, internal account reference, service point, meter or aggregate ID, parent meter if applicable |
| Statement lineage | Invoice ID and version, date, source reference and superseded invoice or lines |
| Service period | Start, end, day count, and source convention for the end date |
| Billed usage | Kilowatt-hours and read status: actual, estimated or unspecified |
| Demand | Billed kilowatts if shown, and the billing definition or terms reference |
| Charges | Current-period supply, delivery, demand, fixed charges and taxes where itemized. Record earlier-period adjustments and amount due separately. |
| Comparison | Prior service period, usable daily average, verified coverage, comparison group and matched area if applicable |

## Decide whether the row can be compared

| Check | Disposition |
| --- | --- |
| Matched scope | Comparable when dates align and usage evidence supports the stated period and peer group. |
| Estimate or allocation | Provisional if an estimated read or a day-based allocation limits confidence. Show the reason and who will recheck it. |
| Gap or scope change | Held if a material scope change, gap, overlap or unknown load prevents comparison. Display the invoice and usage separately with the exception. |
| Correction | Retain both versions. Verify the replacement scope, recalculate affected periods and issue a dated report revision. |

## Assign the next action

- [ ] Send charge and prior-period discrepancies to the billing or accounts payable owner.
- [ ] Send meter, service-area or duplicate coverage questions to the utility account owner and facilities verifier.
- [ ] Send a validated usage variance to the site or engineering lead with the dates, boundary and operating context.
- [ ] Log the responsible person, due date, required closeout evidence, report revision and recipients who relied on the original.

## Worked example — explicitly fictional

| Cobalt Marsh Storage | Reconciliation |
| --- | --- |
| Earlier statement | $2,400 total, 12,000 kWh, 30 service days, 400 kWh/day |
| Later statement | $3,540 total, 14,000 kWh, 35 service days, 400 kWh/day, earlier-period adjustment shown |
| Change in coverage | An expansion behind the meter opened during the later cycle. Its effective date remains to be confirmed. |
| Disposition | Hold the like-for-like comparison. Retain both billed amounts and usage quantities with a coverage note. |
| Closeout evidence | Billing owner reconciles adjustment to provider records. Facilities verifies the effective date and the report owner prepares a matched-scope comparison. |
