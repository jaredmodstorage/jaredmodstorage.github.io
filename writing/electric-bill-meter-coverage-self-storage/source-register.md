# Source register

Accessed November 24, 2026. Direct retrieval returned HTTP 200 for all four. The portfolio workflow and example are authored recommendations.

| Source and provenance | Use and material limitation |
| --- | --- |
| [EIA, Measuring Electricity](https://www.eia.gov/energyexplained/electricity/measuring-electricity.php) · U.S. federal energy statistics agency | Distinguishes kW from kWh and describes utility meter measurement. The source does not define any example site's demand charge or terms. |
| [EPA ENERGY STAR, How to Get Utility Data into Portfolio Manager](https://www.energystar.gov/sites/default/files/2025-01/How%20to%20Get%20utility%20data%20into%20Portfolio%20Manager_December%202024.pdf) · EPA product guide, December 2024 | Meter selection and coverage, estimation fields, gaps and overlaps. The product's validation behavior is distinct from the proposed internal controls. |
| [Con Edison, Your Guide to Rates](https://www.coned.com/en/accounts-billing/your-bill/your-guide-to-rates) · first-party U.S. electric utility glossary | Example of provider-labeled actual and estimated reads. The definition and correction handling on any given bill depend on its own utility and applicable terms. |
| [EPA ENERGY STAR, Analyze Benchmarking Results](https://www.energystar.gov/buildings/benchmark/analyze-benchmarking-results) · EPA program guidance | Grouping properties by characteristics such as function and metering and comparing historical periods. Gives no storage-specific benchmark, score eligibility, certification finding or verified savings. |
