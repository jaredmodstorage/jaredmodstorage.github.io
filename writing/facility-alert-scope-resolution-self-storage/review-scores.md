- Opens with a recognizable multi-location failure and reaches the source-versus-scope distinction immediately.
- Maintains a direct systems-operator voice without generic AI framing, fake quotations, or inflated outcomes.
- Connects public alert structure to facility identity, portfolio workflow, updates, and operating release.

- Gives regional teams a usable progression: freeze source, preserve geography, load governed locations, resolve per facility, assign bounded work, track readback, recompute.

- Does not convert an alert match into automatic closure or a claimed site condition.
