# Vehicle Leak Boundary and Release Card

Use this card for an observed liquid trail or puddle associated with a vehicle route. It is an operating record, not a material-identification or cleanup procedure.

| Gate | Required record | Hold or escalate when | Release evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Discovery | Event ID, time, observer, exact location, visible footprint | Location or extent cannot be observed safely | Observation recorded from outside the boundary |
| People | Contact, symptom and exposure reports; accountability state | Fire, smoke, vapor, symptoms, contact, collision or immediate danger | Emergency/public-authority branch resolved under the site plan |
| Route | Affected pedestrian, cart and vehicle paths; barriers; alternate route | Any approach remains uncontrolled or alternate route is unsafe | Every approach is controlled and customer instructions are active |
| Substance state | Neutral description; identity `unknown`, `reported` or `qualified-confirmed` | Identification would require touching, smelling, sampling or guessing | Qualified owner controls any identification needed for response |
| Source | Vehicle/container description and active-leak state | Source is unsafe, moving, actively releasing or unverified | Source handled through its separate qualified workstream |
| Water path | Nearest drain, slope, rain/weather and observed migration | Material reaches or threatens a drain or water path | Environmental/emergency owner directs the required control and follow-up |
| Response | Named response owner, dispatch/ticket, procedure and permitted method | Trained role, approved method, materials or authority is missing | Qualified/authorized owner records response completion or continuing restriction |
| Customer commitments | Affected access, appointments, move-ins, deliveries and next update | Customer must cross the boundary or route state is unclear | Commitments are updated, rerouted, rescheduled or explicitly held |
| Waste | Used response media, container, custody, storage and disposition owner | Waste route, container or ownership is unknown | Disposition responsibility and next action are recorded |
| Reopening | Surface, tracking, drain path, barriers, systems and communication readback | Any release condition remains unsupported | Named release owner records decision, evidence, time and open follow-up |

## Required state vocabulary

- `unknown`: the fact has not been established; it does not authorize action.
- `observed`: a person recorded a direct condition without diagnosis.
- `reported`: a statement was received but not independently verified.
- `restricted`: the route or service remains unavailable.
- `escalated`: a named qualified or authorized owner has the next decision.
- `responding`: the approved response is active; reopening is not implied.
- `released`: the named operating-release conditions were verified.
- `closed_with_follow_up`: traffic may resume while a separately identified action remains open.

The companion CSV contains the implementable blank record. The fictional example is labeled and must not be used as evidence of a real event or as a substitute for a site-specific emergency, environmental or safety plan.
