# Unlocked-Office Opening Hold and Handoff Card

Use this card when a staffed office is unexpectedly open, ajar or unlocked before normal opening. It records a safe first observation and the authorized release path. It does not replace the site's emergency plan, law-enforcement direction, legal advice or qualified security response.

## Immediate branch

- [ ] Move to a safe position outside the office.
- [ ] If an unknown person, forced-entry activity, weapon, smoke, medical emergency or other immediate danger is present, call 911 and follow responder instructions.
- [ ] If no immediate threat is observed, keep the approach clear and contact the approved security or operations owner.
- [ ] Do not enter, close or lock the door, try the handle repeatedly, move nearby items or investigate.
- [ ] Pause office-dependent services; use only the preapproved alternate customer path.
