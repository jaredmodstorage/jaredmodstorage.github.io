# Exit-Sign and Route-Visibility Control Card

Use this card when a facility employee observes an exit sign, directional marking or related route-visibility condition that may be impaired. It is an operating record, not an electrical diagnosis or a code inspection.

## 1. Identify the observation

- Record incident ID, facility ID, observer role and time.
- Identify the exact device, device type, route and location.
- State what was observed in plain language.
- Record the observation source and evidence references.
- Leave cause, power state and technical diagnosis unknown unless established by an authorized source.

## 2. Establish the route state

- Record sign-face visibility, line of sight and ambient lighting as observed.
- Identify the governing emergency-plan reference and designated decision role.
- Set the affected area to **normal**, **controlled exception** or **unavailable** only through the authorized site process.
- If an alternate route is used, record the exact route and who verified it.

## 3. Apply approved controls

- Record customer and employee access boundaries.
- Record each interim control and its approving role.
- Do not improvise signs, block exits, narrow a route, hold fire doors open or perform unqualified electrical work.
- Give staff the current state, allowed actions, prohibited actions, owner and next update time.

## 4. Escalate and service

- Name the required notification role and the qualified service provider.
- Record the service ticket and provider arrival or response evidence.
- Keep reported work separate from verified test results.

## 5. Release on evidence

- Record the approved test method or authority.
- Record who performed the test, when, and the exact result.
- Attach repair, test and route-walk evidence required by the site.
- Record the release authority, release decision and release time.
- Do not close the record while the route state or follow-up ownership is unknown.

## State definitions

- **Normal:** Current release evidence supports the site-defined normal operating condition.
- **Controlled exception:** An authorized exception procedure, access boundary, owner and next-update time are active.
- **Unavailable:** Normal use of the affected area is not authorized.
- **Unknown:** A fact has not been established. Unknown never converts itself to normal.
