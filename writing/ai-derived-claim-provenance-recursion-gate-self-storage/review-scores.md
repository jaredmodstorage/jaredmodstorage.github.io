# Editorial review scores

Scale: 1–5. Any score below 4 blocks handoff.

| Dimension | Score | Note |
|---|---:|---|
| Advanced architecture | 5 | Governs claim graphs, evidence classes, derivation activities, recursion and reverse correction indexes. |
| Operator consequence | 5 | Shows how a sourced regional briefing can still lose its route to facility evidence. |
| Human authority | 5 | Defines a specific handback with blocked claim, broken edge, required evidence and accountable role. |
| Semantic safety | 5 | Prevents plans, assertions, generated interpretations and observations from collapsing into one class. |
| Practical utility | 5 | Includes a 55-field gate, blank template and fictional pass/block records. |
| Originality | 5 | Distinct from retrieval coverage, context truncation, explanation fidelity and known-source correction propagation. |
| Claim discipline | 5 | Sources are primary and their applicability limits are explicit. No implementation or result is invented. |
| Voice and readiness | 5 | Mature, direct, technically specific and free of generic AI commentary. |

**Result:** Pass, subject to final word count, CSV validation, originality scan, checksum freeze and publisher live-readback.
