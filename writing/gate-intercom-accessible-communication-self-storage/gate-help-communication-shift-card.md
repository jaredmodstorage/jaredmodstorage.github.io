# Gate Help and Communication Shift Card

Use this card from the gate approach, the office and the after-hours support desk. Fill in the site’s actual routes and contacts before a drill.

## Prepare the route

| Confirm | Record |
| --- | --- |
| Visible direction | Where the customer first sees an alternate contact method and the way to a safe waiting location |
| Live contact | Approved communication channels, staffed hours and who answers each one |
| After-hours help | Published route, who owns it and who can arrange additional support |
| Private verification | Accessible verification method and the duty manager who can approve an equivalent path |
| Service finish | How the team will confirm the customer received directions and completed the next service step |

## Work the request

- [ ] Give a safe next direction and use a communication method that works for the customer.
- [ ] Ask what help is needed. Confirm the request and next step directly with the customer.
- [ ] Move account questions and verification to an approved private channel.
- [ ] Confirm the service step or assign a person and time for the next update.

## Test the route

| Drill condition | Passing evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Voice intercom unusable | Alternate route is visible and reaches a responder without requiring the same voice exchange. |
| No usable mobile data | The duty manager can identify a usable planned route or arrange an effective alternative with a named owner. |
| Written directions insufficient | Staff can arrange more suitable assistance and give the customer a usable update. |
| Relay call | The call is handled through the normal service queue and its verification steps, including any transfer. |

## Worked example — explicitly fictional

| Alder Reach Storage | Record |
| --- | --- |
| Customer need | Move-in appointment. Voice intercom reply could not be heard. |
| Channel | Customer uses the staffed business text number from the move-in instructions while stopped in the designated area. |
| Service state | Directions confirmed. Staff complete private verification and support entry under the normal gate procedure. |
| Correction | Alternate contact number was hard to see. The shift lead assigns the sign correction and a new approach drill. |
