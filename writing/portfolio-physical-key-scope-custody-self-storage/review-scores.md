# Editorial review scores

Scale: 1–5. A score below 4 blocks handoff.

| Dimension | Score | Editorial note |
|---|---:|---|
| Operator consequence | 5 | Turns a familiar ring-and-label problem into a portfolio control with accountable exceptions. |
| Multi-location systems depth | 5 | Separates identity, scope, custody, authorization, duplication, lock lineage and lifecycle. |
| Next-step usefulness | 5 | Includes a field-level register, CSV template and fictional worked dataset. |
| Originality | 5 | Physical-key scope/custody is distinct from same-day lock removal and prior digital access articles. |
| Claim discipline | 5 | Federal sources are correctly bounded; unknowns and fictional facts remain explicit. |
| Voice | 5 | Direct, mature and operational without generic AI language or fake quotations. |
| Source quality | 5 | Uses current primary federal publications and records their applicability limits. |
| Publication readiness | 5 | Single canonical, governed image, disclosures, source register and publisher boundary are specified. |

**Result:** Pass, subject to final mechanical word count, CSV validation, checksum freeze and live publisher preflight.
