- Cedar Lantern Storage, all six facilities, the 600-record population, every exception count, the sample, sentinel, versions, and result are explicitly fictional teaching data.
- Model output, lack of output, telemetry, facility observation, reviewer interpretation, and final operating evidence remain separate states.
- An empty queue is not represented as proof that a facility is safe or normal.
- OpenTelemetry supports the described trace structure; it is not used as evidence of data completeness, model correctness, facility truth, or safe operating policy.
- NIST AI RMF material is voluntary, cross-industry guidance and is not represented as a self-storage rule, certification, or endorsement.
- Negative review is described as a bounded sample with its population and evidence; no sampled result is generalized into an unsupported performance claim.
- Sentinels are expressly non-consequential and do not authorize customer contact, access changes, vendor dispatch, charges, or mutation of a real facility record.
- The AI-generated image is disclosed as illustrative editorial media and not documentary evidence.
- Draft completion, publisher handoff, repository integration, deployment, public readback, indexing, coverage, and recognition remain distinct states.
