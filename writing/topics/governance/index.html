Public and operating records bounded by proof.

Methods for keeping public facts, platform permissions, legal relationships, and operating identities separate enough to verify, correct, and govern.

Topic boundary

What belongs in this collection.

This topic distinguishes observable public facts, administrative access, and legal or operational relationships. A login, logo, directory record, or schema assertion does not establish ownership by itself.
