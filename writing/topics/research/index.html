Methods and evidence before conclusions.

Release methods for numbers and case studies that name the source, period, population, measure, limitations, uncertainty, and permission boundary.

Topic boundary

What belongs in this collection.

These methods explain how a claim should be tested and released. They do not supply a benchmark result, customer outcome, portfolio comparison, financial result, or independent validation.

  1. 01
    Is the claim defined before the data is selected?
  2. 02
    Are population, period, unit, denominator, and uncertainty visible?
  3. 03
    Can another reviewer reproduce the release decision?
