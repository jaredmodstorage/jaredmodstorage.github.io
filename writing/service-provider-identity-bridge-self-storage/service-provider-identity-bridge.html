Service-Provider Identity Bridge

Create one row for one source record and one exact provider relationship. The downloadable CSV includes one unfilled row and four explicitly fictional examples. This browser field guide does not save or transmit entered information.

Names are candidates, not authority. Do not place bank, tax, credential, personal or sensitive security data here. This bridge does not authorize vendors, payments, access, contracts, insurance, ownership or performance conclusions.

1. Anchor the portfolio record
2. Preserve source-local identity
3. Record legal and engagement evidence
4. Decide the relationship
5. Bound use and preserve evidence
Readback rule: a confirmed link supports only the named use. It does not silently authorize payment, access, contracting, tax, insurance, ownership or performance conclusions.

Authored field guide informed by Open Contracting identifier guidance, GLEIF's LEI description and NIST SP 1305. It is not an agency or standards-body form. See source-register.md for limits.
