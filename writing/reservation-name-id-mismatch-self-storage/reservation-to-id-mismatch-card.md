# Reservation-to-ID Mismatch Hold and Resolution Card

Use one card per unresolved reservation-to-identity mismatch. Follow the facility's current accepted-evidence policy, rental agreement, privacy rules and escalation path. This card is not an accepted-document list, fraud determination, legal opinion or authorization to collect unnecessary personal data.
