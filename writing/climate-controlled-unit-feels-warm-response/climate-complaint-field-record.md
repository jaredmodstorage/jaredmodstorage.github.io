# Climate Complaint Field Record

For one reported condition at one named unit or zone. Copy into the facility's approved ticket system or print. Enter `unknown` where a fact has not been established; an empty field is not a normal result. Follow the site emergency procedure immediately for a safety condition. This record does not authorize a customer-unit entry, technical repair or claim about stored property.
