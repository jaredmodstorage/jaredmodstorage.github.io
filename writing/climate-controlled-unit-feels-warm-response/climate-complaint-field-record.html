Climate Complaint Field Record

One reported condition at one named unit or zone. Copy completed answers into the facility's approved incident system or print this blank record. This file does not save or transmit entered information.

Use unknown when a fact is not established; a blank field is not a normal result. Follow the site emergency procedure immediately for a safety condition. This record does not authorize customer-unit entry, technical repair, a claim about stored property, or a temperature guarantee.

1. Customer report
2. Actual promise
3. Place and direct observation
4. System evidence and footprint
5. Operating decision
6. Qualified technical handoff
7. Customer update
8. Closure readbacks
Readback rule: a normal controller status, completed technician visit or sent customer message alone cannot close the reported unit condition. Record the affected place, source, time and named release decision separately.

Authored operating template informed by EPA's building complaint-investigation guide. It is not an EPA form, a site-specific emergency plan or a legal standard. See source-register.md for scope limits.
