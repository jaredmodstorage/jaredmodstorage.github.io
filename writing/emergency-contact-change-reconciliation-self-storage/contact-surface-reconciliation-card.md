# Emergency Contact Surface Reconciliation Card

Use one change episode for one authorized assignment change. Create one register row for every facility, contact purpose, and internal or external surface that can route the contact.

## 1. Authorize the role change

- Change episode ID
- Trigger and governing source
- Old role assignment
- New role assignment
- Facility scope
- Contact purposes
- Authority granted
- Effective start and expiration
- Privacy/channel restrictions
- Change owner

## 2. Inventory the surfaces

- Emergency plan or posted call tree
- Internal on-call directory
- Monitoring or service provider account
- Device or platform notification profile
- Work-order or incident system
- Printed binder, wallet card, or lockbox sheet
- Approved local exception
- Surface owner and update method

## 3. Track each state separately

`not applicable` → `identified` → `requested` → `acknowledged` → `changed` → `read back` → `exercised` → `reconciled`

Use `failed` when the wrong or incomplete route appears. A request receipt is not a saved-value readback.

## 4. Verify and exercise

- Read back the saved name, role, channel, sequence, facility, purpose, and effective time.
- Use only an approved nonemergency test that cannot be confused with an actual alarm.
- Record the first recipient reached, actual sequence, acknowledgment, unexpected recipient, and stop condition.
- Preserve limitations where a provider does not expose the saved value or support a safe test.

## 5. Close the old route

- Removed, expired, disabled, history-only, or approved temporary overlap
- Evidence of old-route disposition
- Corrective owner and retest date for every failed row
- Episode close authority and timestamp

The episode closes only when every required row is reconciled or explicitly marked not applicable by an authorized owner.
