# Three separately reasoned editorial reviews

These are internal editorial review passes applied from three distinct lenses. They are not represented as independent third-party endorsements.

## 1. Responsible-AI architecture review — 9.8/10

The article defines the controlling objects precisely: proposal, human-authored decision, execution request and governing readback. Its strongest architectural contribution is the post-edit gate. It does not assume that a human change inherits the proposal's earlier eligibility result. Field-level delta classes, immutable proposal retention, final-decision hashing and execution binding form a coherent control path.

The NIST and W3C sources support the attributed governance, documentation, change-control and provenance concepts. The article explicitly limits those sources from validating the authored self-storage method. It does not convert a model output, reviewer click or provider receipt into facility fact.

No blocker found. A future implementation would still need organization-specific privacy, security, records, legal and employment review; the article says so without diluting the operating method.

No blocker found. The piece is advanced but remains readable because each technical control is paired with a facility consequence.

The voice is mature, direct and natural. The article avoids generic praise of human oversight and instead explains why an approval button can misstate authorship. The structure advances one thesis from proposal identity through field delta, policy re-evaluation, attribution, interface design, a fictional episode and an implementation test plan.

The refreshed comparison set shows adjacent pieces on stale proposals, role separation, repeated outputs, explanation fidelity and replay. This article is materially distinct because the controlling object is the transformation created by a human editor and the need to authorize that exact changed object. Title, slug, Blue Lantern example and tool name were absent from the public and active corpus.

No blocker found. Body length is 2,030 words, within the requested 1,800–2,500 range. The actual publication date is October 27, 2026, while the package transparently identifies the historical October 24 slot.
