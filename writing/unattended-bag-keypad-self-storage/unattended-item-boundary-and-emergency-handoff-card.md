# Unattended Item Boundary and Emergency Handoff Card

Use this card with the facility emergency plan and local authority instructions. It does not replace emergency services, law enforcement direction, site-specific training or legal requirements.

## Immediate boundary

1. **Do not touch, tamper with or move the item.** Do not ask another person to inspect it.
2. **Stop the approach.** Move staff, customers and vendors away using the safest known route. Do not route people past the item.
3. **Activate the facility emergency plan.** For an emergency, call 911. Follow responding-authority instructions.
4. **Name one operator contact.** This person maintains the log and briefs responders from a safe position.
