# Customer Lock-Removal Authorization and Custody Card

Use this card when a customer asks the facility to remove a lock or other locking device from a unit. It is a decision-and-evidence aid, not permission to enter a unit, handle contents, remove a facility control, operate a tool outside training or decide a legal dispute.

## Claim boundary

This record documents the cited workflow and observations. It does not, by itself, prove ownership of stored goods, legal authority beyond the cited source, condition or completeness of contents, absence of tampering, compliance, or security against every threat. Blank and `unknown` fields do not authorize work.
