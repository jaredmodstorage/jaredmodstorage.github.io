## Session 1 — Governing Facility Identity as an Operating Control

Facility identity should be treated as an operating control, not a marketing cleanup. This session shows multi-location operators how to build an 18-field source register, separate public identity from platform access and legal relationships, classify active and legacy records, assign evidence owners and approvers, execute an eight-step correction workflow, and verify visible outcomes.

Learning outcomes:

1. Build an 18-field source register for each facility.
2. Separate public identity, platform access, and legal relationships.
3. Use an eight-step correction and visible-verification loop.

Published method: https://jaredmodstorage.github.io/writing/facility-identity-drift/

Applied tool: https://jaredmodstorage.github.io/downloads/facility-identity-control-self-assessment.xlsx

Full sample presentation: https://jaredmodstorage.github.io/downloads/governing-facility-identity-operating-control.pptx

## Session 2 — Evidence Before Automation

A decision boundary for teams adopting AI without allowing a suggestion, approval, action, and reconciled outcome to collapse into the same claim.

Learning outcomes:

1. Identify the source and freshness required for a decision.
2. Design explicit authority and approval boundaries.
3. Separate operational progress from verified outcomes.

Published method: https://jaredmodstorage.github.io/writing/evidence-before-automation/

## Session 3 — From Dashboards to a Governed Operating System

A vendor-neutral examination of why visibility alone is not an operating system and what accountable execution requires after a dashboard reveals a condition.

Learning outcomes:

1. Distinguish reporting, workflow, approval, action, and proof.
2. Map exceptions to named owners and recovery paths.
3. Evaluate operating software without confusing roadmap and release status.

Published method: https://jaredmodstorage.github.io/writing/operating-system-not-dashboard/

## Session 4 — Publishing an Operations Case Study That Survives Scrutiny

A reproducible method for turning operating activity into a bounded case study with defined periods, denominators, interventions, exceptions, reconciliation, privacy review, and limitations.

Learning outcomes:

1. Frame one answerable operating question.
2. Preserve numerator, denominator, comparison, and exception evidence.
3. Apply review, privacy, limitation, and publication gates.

Published method: https://jaredmodstorage.github.io/writing/case-study-that-survives-scrutiny/
