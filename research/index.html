Publish the method before the conclusion.

This public research surface explains how operating questions become supportable publications. Methods, frameworks, and proposed standards are available now. Company-specific performance findings remain unpublished until source, privacy, reconciliation, review, and permission gates are complete.

Evidence state

Methods public.
Results gated.

No company-specific result is cleared on this page. A planned or discussed study is not an executed study; an executed analysis is not reconciled; and a reconciled result is not public until the responsible source owner and reviewer approve its release.

Owned research can document a method or a measured result. It does not become independent coverage, market leadership, customer proof, certification, or evidence of encyclopedic notability.

02

Publication ladder

01

Method note

Publishes a process, definitions, and limits. It does not report a company result.

02

Descriptive report

Documents a defined population or period without making a causal claim.

03

Comparative benchmark

Uses a declared comparison, consistent definitions, and an auditable denominator.

04

Measured case study

Adds an intervention, reconciliation proof, privacy review, limitations, and publication permission.

05

Independent study

Requires an editorially and analytically independent party; owned work cannot self-assign this label.
