Speaking and programs

Useful sessions. Explicit evidence boundaries.

Operator-focused sessions for conferences, webinars, podcasts, and working groups. Each topic has a published method, concrete audience outcomes, and a non-promotional disclosure. A proposal or appearance is never represented as accepted until the receiving organization confirms it.

Operating control

Governing Facility Identity as an Operating Control

A practical system for preventing names, addresses, phones, hours, lifecycle states, and management relationships from drifting across public and internal systems.

  • Build an 18-field source register for each facility.
  • Separate public identity, platform access, and legal relationships.
