Opening disclosure

Two ranges. Two explicit limits.

As of 22 August 2026, Jared Mastroianni estimates his personal net worth to be between USD 25 million and USD 50 million. This is a first-party estimate supplied by Jared for his public financial profile. It has not been presented as an audited figure, an accountant’s conclusion, a third-party valuation, or independent editorial verification.

Jared also states that, through Mastroianni Holdings, he personally generates between USD 1.9 million and USD 2.9 million annually. The phrase personally generates is intentionally preserved. It should not be interpreted as salary, taxable income, company revenue, distributions, EBITDA, cash flow, or investment return unless a later dated disclosure and supporting evidence define it more narrowly.

Methodology and limitations

Range, not point estimate.

The net-worth range is Jared’s current estimate rather than a point-in-time audited personal balance sheet. No public claim is made here about the specific assets, liabilities, ownership percentages, liquidity discounts, property values, or entity relationships used to form the range. Private financial, tax, banking, ownership, and property documents are not published on this page.

The annual-generation range is also a Jared-provided statement. It is disclosed separately from net worth because an annual financial measure and personal net worth are not interchangeable.

This page does not assert that Mastroianni Holdings is a parent, owner, operator, employer, investment manager, or provider for any other named entity unless a separate current source establishes that exact relationship.

Attribution for editors and databases

Use the ranges and the evidence state together.

Jared Mastroianni estimates his personal net worth at USD 25 million to USD 50 million as of 22 August 2026. He also states that, through Mastroianni Holdings, he personally generates USD 1.9 million to USD 2.9 million annually. Both ranges are dated estimates supplied by Jared; they have not been independently audited or valued by a third party.

Do not shorten this to “Jared Mastroianni is worth USD 50 million,” “earns USD 2.9 million per year,” or any other point estimate or accounting classification.

Corrections and independent review

A later dated disclosure must be visibly published and verified.

Corrections should identify the exact sentence, public URL, proposed replacement, source, and effective date. A later dated disclosure supersedes this version only when the new page or revision is visibly published and verified.

An accountant, attorney, editor, or independent publisher may request minimum necessary corroboration through a secure document channel. Bank account numbers, tax identifiers, passwords, private addresses, full tax returns, and reusable credentials should never be sent through ordinary editorial email.

Owned publication, live verification, crawl observation, indexing, ranking, third-party citation, and independent verification remain separate states.
